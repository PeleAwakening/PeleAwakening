Our Story

About Pele Awakening

A platform for curated wellness experiences in Hawaiʻi, rooted in nature, ancient wisdom, and cultural respect.

We facilitate a convergence — where Eastern ancient wisdom meets the elemental power of the Hawaiian ʻĀina, using Tai Chi and TCM to channel Pele’s primal fire of transformation.

Not a Class. An Experience.

Why We Exist

Pele Awakening began with a simple observation: the most meaningful wellness moments rarely happen in a single class. They happen in immersion — when movement, therapy, nature, and human connection are woven together over real time, in a real place.

So instead of building a studio around one practice, we built a platform around a philosophy: Nature, Holistic Therapy, Traditional Chinese Medicine, Tai Chi & Qigong, Mindfulness, Retreats, and Community — brought together on Oʻahu, and guided by practitioners we trust.

Honoring the legendary Hawaiian deity of fire and volcanic power.

A symbol of transformation and cultural resilience — and the namesake we hold with respect and gratitude.

Pele is the living fire.

Ka ʻĀina a Pele — Land of Pele
Our Vision

A Platform for Curated Wellness

Our long-term vision is to become the home for curated wellness experiences in Hawaiʻi — a place where a small, trusted circle of practitioners, therapists, and guides each bring their own expertise to a shared foundation of nature, tradition, and care.
