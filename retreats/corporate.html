Corporate Retreat

Team wellness and connection, built for your group.

Start Planning

A Corporate Retreat brings your team off-site and into a shared, unhurried experience — group movement, guided therapy, and time in nature that builds connection without a packed agenda. We work with you to shape a day, or several, around your team’s size and goals.

What’s Included

Choose From

  • Group Tai Chi or Kung Fu session — a shared practice for the whole team
  • Guided meditation & breathwork — a reset between work sessions
  • Therapy add-ons — acupuncture, cupping, or massage for the group
  • Ocean or nature outing — paddleboarding, hiking, or a beach gathering
  • Private venue & catering coordination — on request
  • Flexible scheduling — half-day, full-day, or multi-day formats
