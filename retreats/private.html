Private Retreat

A fully bespoke journey, built around you.

Bespoke by Design

Your Dates, Your Group, Your Intentions

Some journeys don’t fit a set schedule. A Private Retreat is built entirely around you — a family, a team, a small circle of friends, or a single individual — with flexible dates, a personalized itinerary, and dedicated time with our practitioners.

Every element of the 3-Day and 5-Day retreats — Experiences, Therapy, guided movement, and quiet reflection — can be drawn on and reshaped to suit your group’s pace and purpose.

What’s Included

Designed Around You

  • Flexible dates — scheduled around your calendar, not ours.
  • Custom itinerary — drawn from any Experience, Therapy, or retreat element.
  • Dedicated practitioner time — sessions built for your group alone.
  • Private setting — an intimate, uninterrupted experience on Oʻahu.
