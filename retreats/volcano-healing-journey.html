Signature Retreat

Volcano Healing Journey

The complete journey — arrival to rebirth, at an unhurried pace.

Channel the primal energy of the Volcano Goddess Pele. Five days of profound personal transformation, where the molten fire of the earth burns away old burdens, and the cool water of the ocean integrates the new self. Arrive for release, depart with new energy and unparalleled clarity.

The Path

Five Days

01

Arrival & Grounding

Connect with the island’s pulse. Feel the ancient earth beneath your feet as you arrive and release the weight of the outside world.

02

Ignition

Burn away the old self. Through movement, breathwork, and volcanic energy, ignite the transformation within.

03

Alchemizing

Fuse Eastern & Western healing. Traditional Chinese Medicine meets Hawaiian tradition in a powerful synthesis of body and spirit.

04

Integration

Let the work settle. A slower day of therapy, rest, and reflection, allowing the transformation to take root.

05

Rebirth

Emerge as the living fire. Carry the transformative power of Pele into your renewed life with clarity, strength, and purpose.

Duration 5 Days / 4 Nights
Location Oʻahu, Hawaiʻi
Group Size 12 Participants Max
