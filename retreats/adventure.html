Adventure Retreat

Surf, dive, hike, and explore Oʻahu’s wild edges.

An Adventure Retreat is built for movement — ocean, trail, and everything in between. We combine Oʻahu’s natural landscape with the same grounding practices that run through every Pele Awakening experience, so the days stay active without losing their center.
