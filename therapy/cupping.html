Therapy

Cupping

Gentle suction therapy that eases tension and encourages circulation.

What It Is

A Practice of Release

Cupping uses gentle suction — traditionally through heated or pump-drawn glass or silicone cups — placed against the skin to draw tension toward the surface and encourage circulation. It is one of the oldest bodywork practices in Traditional Chinese Medicine, valued for the deep sense of release it leaves behind.

The Experience

At Pele Awakening

Each session is tailored to areas of tightness or fatigue, often following a day of movement or travel. It pairs especially well with Sunrise Tai Chi or a Volcano Journey, easing the body after activity.

  • Muscle release — easing tightness in the back, shoulders, and legs.
  • Improved circulation — encouraging blood flow to tired muscles.
  • Deep relaxation — a distinct, grounding sense of release.
