What It Is

Tai Chi in the Hands

Tai Chi Massage applies the body mechanics of Tai Chi directly to bodywork: intention guides Qi, and Qi guides strength. Working from point, to line, to area, the practitioner follows the body’s meridians to guide the flow of Qi and blood — promoting balanced energy distribution, improving circulation, and supporting the body’s own metabolism, self-healing, and immune function.

The technique is natural and unforced, matched to the body’s own rhythms — deep and steady pressure with an unbroken sense of flow.
