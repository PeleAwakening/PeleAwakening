A Lifetime of Practice

Master Wang Guolong is a registered therapist in Hawaiʻi with more than 30 years of clinical experience in Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM), therapeutic bodywork, and traditional Chinese martial arts.

Born in Harbin, China, he began training in traditional martial arts at the age of nine and later studied Chinese medicine under his grandfather. Over decades of dedicated practice, he has seamlessly integrated the principles of Tai Chi, martial arts, and Chinese medicine into a therapeutic philosophy that emphasizes natural movement, internal energy (Qi), and holistic recovery.

Today, he continues his clinical work in Hawaiʻi, helping students and clients improve mobility, reduce pain, relieve stress, and restore overall well-being.

Therapeutic Philosophy

The Flow of Qi & Strength

Master Wang’s therapeutic bodywork combines the principles of Traditional Chinese Medicine with the internal mechanics of Tai Chi. Rather than relying solely on muscular force, his treatment emphasizes whole-body movement, structural alignment, and the flow of Qi through the body’s meridian system. Each session is intuitively customized to the client’s condition.

Circulation

Improve energy distribution and blood flow.

Mission & Community

Cultural Exchange & Lifelong Wellness

For more than three decades, Master Wang has dedicated his life to preserving and sharing the traditions of Chinese medicine, Tai Chi, Daoist health cultivation, and martial arts. His mission is to help people reconnect body and mind through time-tested healing practices, while promoting cultural exchange and elevating the standing of the Chinese American community he serves.
