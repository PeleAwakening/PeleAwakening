Registered Therapist & Martial Arts Lineage Holder

Wang Guolong

Shifu · Daoist name Shi Chu (師初)

Traditional Chinese medicine and martial arts, carried through five generations of lineage.

Book a Session

Wang Guolong, known by the Daoist name Shi Chu, was born in Harbin, China, and is now a registered therapist in Hawaiʻi. He began training in traditional Chinese martial arts at age nine, and at seventeen began studying medicine under his grandfather, Lou Wang.

Lineage & Training

A Lineage Five Generations Deep

Shifu Wang trained under Master Fang Jincheng, Shifu Sun Liangshan, Dr. Sun Junxian, and Daoist priest Gongsun Qinggao. He is recognized as:

  • Tenth-generation authentic heir — Wang Lang Praying Mantis Boxing (王郎螳螂拳)
  • Sixth-generation authentic heir — Haojia Men Taiji Meihua Praying Mantis Boxing (郝家門太極梅花螳螂拳)
  • Fifteenth-generation authentic successor — Wudang Sanfeng Natural Sect (武當三峰自然派)
  • Third-generation heir — the Lou family’s classical acupuncture technique, from renowned physician Lou Baiceng
Recognition

Competition History

  • 1991 — Harbin City Boxing Champion, 57kg division
  • 1993 — Harbin City Boxing Champion, 71kg division
  • 2007 — Heilongjiang Province Traditional Martial Arts Competition, Gold Medal (Weapons)
  • 2022 — NTD Chinese Traditional Martial Arts Competition, New York, Bronze Medal (Weapons)
  • 2024 — NTD Chinese Traditional Martial Arts Competition, New York, Bronze Medal (Forms)
Practice Today

From the Mariana Islands to Hawaiʻi

Since 1998, Shifu Wang has practiced medicine and taught traditional Chinese martial arts, first in the Northern Mariana Islands and now on Oʻahu. In 2019, he founded the Hawaii Martial Arts Cultural Association and the Honolulu Zhongmiao Tai Chi Wellness Society, and continues to teach traditional martial arts and Daoist wellness practice at Magic Island.

For decades, he has devoted himself to preserving Traditional Chinese external medicine and martial arts culture, and to elevating the standing of the Chinese American community he serves.
