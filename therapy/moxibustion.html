Therapy

Moxibustion

Warming therapy to invigorate and restore balance.

What It Is

The Warmth of Tradition

Moxibustion uses the gentle, radiant heat of burning dried mugwort (moxa), held close to the skin, to warm and invigorate the body. Practiced alongside acupuncture for centuries, it’s valued for the deep, grounding warmth it brings to the practice.

The Experience

At Pele Awakening

Sessions are calm and unhurried, often paired with acupuncture or bodywork for a fuller sense of warmth and restoration. Many guests find it especially grounding after time in the ocean or open air.

  • Gentle warmth — a soothing, radiant heat applied with care.
  • Invigoration — supporting the body’s sense of vitality.
  • Grounding calm — a deeply settling close to any session.
