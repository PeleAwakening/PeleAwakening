Chronic Pain Back & Neck Pain

From Frequent Epidural Injections to Periodic Maintenance Care

Subject Patricia
Source Client letter, 2011
Reported by The client herself
Prior care Epidural injections every 6–8 weeks

Patricia had lived with chronic back and neck pain for years. Her work required long hours of sitting, and according to her 2011 letter, she had relied on epidural injections approximately every 6–8 weeks for many years.

After relocating to Saipan, she was referred to Guolong Wang. She had never tried acupuncture or Chinese medicine before and wrote that she initially “expected absolutely nothing from the treatment.”

Patricia reported significant relief after her first visit. After approximately 6–7 appointments, she wrote that she only found it necessary to return approximately once every 3–4 months for maintenance care. She also reported that she no longer needed to leave the island for the pain treatments she had previously relied upon.

“Kevin has literally improved the quality of my life in a way that I cannot adequately describe with words.”

— Patricia, in her 2011 letter
Documented

An MRI report Patricia provided documented lumbar degenerative changes, multilevel disc bulging, and an L3–L4 disc herniation. This documents structural findings only — it is not a record of treatment reversing or resolving those findings.

A Family’s Experience

Source Two family letters, 2011
Authors Pamela and Nick Nishikawa
Subjects Pamela, Nick, Natsumi, and Yumi

Two 2011 letters — one from Pamela, one written jointly with her husband Nick Nishikawa — document the experiences of several members of her family who received treatment from Guolong Wang. The accounts below are organized by individual and labeled by who reported them. Both original letters are reproduced at the end of this entry.

02.1 Family member reported

Natsumi

Women’s Health Menstrual Health

According to her mother, Natsumi had experienced years of back and leg pain and was having only about three menstrual periods per year. Her mother wrote that Natsumi had previously taken hormone medication for a year without improvement in the regularity of her cycle.

After a course of 12 treatments with Guolong Wang, her mother reported that Natsumi began having a menstrual period each month and experienced substantially less leg pain.

“After going through 12 treatments with Mr. Kevin Wang she has her menstrual every month and she doesn’t suffer so much of leg pains.”

— Pamela, reporting on her daughter
02.2 Family member reported

Nick Nishikawa

Back & Leg Pain Mobility & Active Recovery

Nick Nishikawa, General Manager of the Hyatt Regency Saipan, had experienced longstanding back and leg injuries. After a few treatments with Guolong Wang, the family reported that he was able to complete a seven-hour marathon in Hawaiʻi and a 10K in Saipan.

02.3 Client reported

Pamela

Whole-Person Wellbeing Back Pain

Pamela described years of significant physical distress before being introduced to Guolong Wang after an acute episode of severe back pain. She reported that her back felt normal after her third treatment and later described major improvements in her sleep, daily functioning, and overall quality of life.

Editor’s note Pamela’s original letter also discusses her broader emotional landscape at the time, including periods of low mood and the role of medication. The account here is intentionally limited to her self-reported experience with back pain, sleep, daily functioning, and quality of life. It is not presented as treatment for any mental-health condition, nor does it suggest that patients should alter or discontinue prescribed medication.

“I am able to lead a normal life, cook, and have another second chance in life again. I feel like 20 years younger.”

— Pamela, in her 2011 letter
02.4 Family member reported

Yumi

Allergies & Digestive Health

Pamela’s younger daughter, Yumi, had allergies and a sensitive stomach from birth. Her mother wrote that after four treatments with Guolong Wang, she was doing well.
