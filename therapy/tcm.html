What It Is

The Foundation of Our Therapy

Traditional Chinese Medicine views the body as a system of interconnected energy pathways, seeking balance rather than treating symptoms in isolation. It’s the philosophy underneath every therapy at Pele Awakening — acupuncture, cupping, moxibustion, and Tai Chi Massage all draw from this same tradition.
