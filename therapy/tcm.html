What It Is

The Foundation of Our Therapy

Traditional Chinese Medicine views the body as a system of interconnected energy pathways, seeking balance rather than treating symptoms in isolation. It’s the philosophy underneath every therapy at Pele Awakening — acupuncture, cupping, moxibustion, and self massage all draw from this same tradition.

The Experience

At Pele Awakening

A TCM consultation is often the starting point for a therapy visit — a conversation to understand what your body needs, before deciding which modality (or combination) fits best.

  • Personalized approach — a conversation before any treatment.
  • Whole-body view — balance sought across the whole system, not just one symptom.
  • Guides every therapy — the thread that ties our therapy offerings together.
