Acupuncture

Fine needles, precisely placed, to restore the body’s natural flow.

What It Is

An Ancient Practice of Balance

Acupuncture is one of the oldest practices within Traditional Chinese Medicine, using fine, sterile needles placed at specific points along the body to encourage balance and ease. At Pele Awakening, it’s offered as a restorative practice woven into your broader wellness experience — not a clinical procedure.

The Experience

At Pele Awakening

Sessions take place in a calm, private setting, beginning with a conversation about how you’re feeling before any treatment begins. Many guests pair acupuncture with a Tai Chi or ocean-based experience for a fuller day of restoration.

  • Ease of tension — encouraging the body to release held stress.
  • Restored balance — supporting the body’s own equilibrium.
  • Deep relaxation — many guests rest or sleep during treatment.
