Not Currently Available

We Want to Be Accurate About What We Offer

Acupuncture requires its own separate license in Hawaiʻi, and we don’t currently offer it. If you’re looking for hands-on traditional Chinese bodywork — cupping, moxibustion, Kung Fu Tuina, and therapeutic massage — Master Wang Guolong, a Hawaiʻi Licensed Massage Therapist (MAT) with over 30 years of experience, offers all of these as part of his practice.
