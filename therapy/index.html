Meet the Practitioner

Master Wang Guolong

Wang Guolong brings more than thirty years of hands-on therapeutic experience, rooted in both a Traditional Chinese Medicine family lineage and a lifetime of martial arts training begun in childhood. His approach draws on traditional bodywork, meridian knowledge, movement, and internal martial arts principles — combined and adapted to what each individual body needs.

Rather than following a fixed protocol, he relies on individualized observation to shape each session — a reflection of traditional knowledge, decades of experience, and an unusual depth of body understanding.
