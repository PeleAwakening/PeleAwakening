Experience

Sunrise Tai Chi

Slow, flowing movement as the island wakes.

There is a particular quality to first light on Oʻahu — still air, soft color, a world not yet in motion. Sunrise Tai Chi meets that quiet directly, using slow, continuous movement to draw the body out of stillness gently, rather than jolting it awake.

Rooted in the same principles that guide Master Jiang’s wider practice, each session builds strength, balance, and focus through form — no prior experience required.

What to Expect

A Grounded Start to the Day

  • Guided form — a sequence of flowing movements suited to all levels.
  • Breath work — coordinated breathing to settle the nervous system.
  • Open-air setting — practiced outdoors, weather and tides permitting.
  • Quiet close — a few minutes of stillness before the day begins.
Duration 60 Minutes
Time of Day Sunrise
Location Oʻahu, Hawaiʻi
