Experience

Ocean Healing

Surrender to the Pacific’s embrace.

Ocean Healing takes the practice to the water’s edge, where the rhythm of the tide becomes a guide for the breath. Time in and near the ocean has long been part of Hawaiian life — here it becomes a structured practice of release, restoration, and reconnection.

Sessions combine guided breathwork, quiet immersion, and reflection, closing with time simply spent by the water.

What to Expect

Restoration at the Water’s Edge

  • Guided breathwork — ocean-paced breathing exercises.
  • Water immersion — time in the shallows, at a comfortable pace for all levels.
  • Quiet reflection — unstructured time by the shoreline.
  • Small groups — an intimate, unhurried setting.
Duration 90 Minutes
Time of Day Late Afternoon
Location Oʻahu, Hawaiʻi
