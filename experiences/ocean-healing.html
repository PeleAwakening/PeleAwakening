Experience

Ocean Discovery

Explore the Pacific’s living reef through guided snorkeling and scuba.

Ocean Discovery brings guests beneath the surface of the Pacific, exploring Oʻahu’s reef through guided snorkeling and introductory scuba. Time in and near the ocean has long been part of Hawaiian life — here, that connection becomes a hands-on, guided adventure beneath the waves.

Sessions are paced for comfort and confidence, whether you’re snorkeling for the first time or trying scuba in a controlled, guided setting. No experience necessary — only curiosity.

What to Expect

Explore the Reef

  • Guided snorkeling — explore Oʻahu’s reef at a comfortable, unhurried pace.
  • Introductory scuba — an optional, guided taste of diving, no certification required.
  • Small groups — personal attention throughout.
  • Beginner-friendly — all gear and guidance included.
Duration 90 Minutes
Time of Day Late Afternoon
Location Oʻahu, Hawaiʻi
