Featured Master, Pele Awakening

Master Jiang

A practice rooted in centuries of tradition, brought to life in Hawaiʻi.

Philosophy

Stillness Within Movement

At the heart of Master Jiang’s practice is a simple belief: that balance is not something we achieve once, but something we return to, breath by breath. Tai Chi and Qigong are approached not as exercise, but as a moving meditation — a way of listening to the body before asking anything of it.

Traditional Chinese Medicine informs this practice throughout, treating movement, breath, and energy as inseparable parts of the same whole.

Teaching Style

Calm, Personal, Attentive

Sessions are guided at an unhurried pace, with attention to each participant’s starting point. Structure provides the form; intuition shapes how it unfolds in the moment — adapting to the group, the setting, and the season.

Areas of Expertise

A Practice Spanning Body and Mind

  • Tai Chi & Qigong — flowing movement for strength, balance, and calm.
  • Traditional Chinese Medicine — foundational principles woven into every session.
  • Meditation & Breathwork — stilling the mind through the breath.
  • Mind-Body Integration — bringing movement, energy, and awareness into one practice.

Join the Experience

A session with Master Jiang typically unfolds outdoors, in small groups, moving between guided practice and quiet reflection. Reach out to plan your session or include it in a retreat.
