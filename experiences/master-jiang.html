International Grandmaster · Featured Master, Pele Awakening

Master Jiang

Heal · Move · Rise — Chen-style Tai Chi rooted in centuries of tradition, brought to life in Hawaiʻi.

Master Jiang is an International Grandmaster dedicated to the profound depths of Chen-style Taijiquan and Qigong. With a lifetime of practice in martial arts and energy cultivation, his teachings transcend mere physical form, emphasizing intention, breath, and inner stillness. As a featured master at Pele Awakening, he guides students toward harmony of body and mind, set against the serene, elemental backdrop of the Hawaiian islands.

Philosophy

Stillness Within Movement

At the heart of Master Jiang’s practice is a simple belief: that balance is not something we achieve once, but something we return to, breath by breath. Chen-style Tai Chi and Qigong are approached not as exercise, but as a moving meditation — a way of listening to the body before asking anything of it.

Traditional Chinese Medicine informs this practice throughout, treating movement, breath, and energy as inseparable parts of the same whole.

Teaching Style

Calm, Personal, Attentive

Sessions are guided at an unhurried pace, with attention to each participant’s starting point. Every gathering closes with Wisdom Sharing & Q&A — time set aside for questions, reflection, and conversation with Master Jiang directly.

  • Chen-Style Taijiquan The original, foundational form of Tai Chi.
  • Push Hands Experience Partnered practice in sensitivity, balance, and yielding.
  • Qigong Practice Breath and energy work to restore and invigorate.
