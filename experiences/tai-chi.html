Weekly Class

Tai Chi

with May

Slow, flowing movement at Magic Island — every Wednesday and Friday morning.

Join a Class
Schedule Wed & Fri, 8–9:30 AM
Location Magic Island, Oʻahu
View on Google Maps →

There is a particular quality to morning air at Magic Island — open lawn, ocean breeze, a world still easing into motion. Tai Chi meets that quiet directly, using slow, continuous movement to draw the body out of stillness gently, rather than jolting it awake.

Held every Wednesday and Friday, 8–9:30 AM, and open to all levels, each class is taught by May, who brings her own dedication to the practice alongside her husband, Wang Guolong — building strength, balance, and focus through form. No prior experience required, and you’re welcome to join any week.

What to Expect

A Grounded Start to the Day

  • Guided form — a sequence of flowing movements suited to all levels.
  • Breath work — coordinated breathing to settle the nervous system.
  • Open-air setting — practiced outdoors at Magic Island, weather permitting.
  • Quiet close — a few minutes of stillness before the day begins.
