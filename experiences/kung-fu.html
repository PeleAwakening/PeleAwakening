Weekly Class

Kung Fu

with Wang Guolong

Traditional martial arts training at Magic Island — every Wednesday and Friday evening.

Join a Class
Schedule Wed & Fri, 5:30–7 PM
Location Magic Island, Oʻahu
View on Google Maps →

Where Tai Chi cultivates softness and stillness, Kung Fu builds power and precision — structured forms, conditioning, and technique drawn from Wang Guolong’s lifelong martial arts training. It’s a more physical counterpart to the rest of Pele Awakening’s practice, without losing the discipline’s meditative core.

Held every Wednesday and Friday, 5:30–7 PM, and open to all levels, each class builds gradually — strength and technique developed over time, not rushed in a single session.

What to Expect

Strength Through Structure

  • Warm-up & conditioning — building the foundation for form work.
  • Forms training — traditional sequences taught step by step.
  • Technique & discipline — precision and control over power.
  • Quiet close — a few minutes of stillness to close the class, at Magic Island as the sun sets.

Join Us at Magic Island

Every Wednesday and Friday, 5:30–7 PM. Drop in, or add it to any retreat.
